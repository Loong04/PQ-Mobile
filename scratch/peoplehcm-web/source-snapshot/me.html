1. PURPOSE & OBJECTIVE OF ROLE

To formulate, direct, and execute Group-wide Human Resource strategies across Times Square Retail Group BHD. The Group HR Manager oversees talent acquisition, executive compensation, payroll administration, employee relations, industrial compliance, and staff development for 12 regional branches and corporate headquarters.

2. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES & ACCOUNTABILITIES
  • Lead Group-wide Talent Acquisition, Workforce Planning, and Executive Onboarding.
  • Manage monthly payroll processing, statutory contributions (EPF, SOCSO, PCB), and annual tax reporting (EA Forms).
  • Oversee industrial relations, grievance handling, and compliance with Malaysia Ministry of Human Resources guidelines.
  • Design and implement Annual Appraisal frameworks, KPI scorecards, and executive bonus allocation multipliers.
  • Direct HR Operations across all 12 retail branches, ensuring consistent policy enforcement and service level agreements (SLAs).
  • Manage HR operational budget, compensation benchmarking, medical benefits insurance, and staff welfare programs.
3. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)
• Executive & Key Staff Retention Rate: ≥ 94.0% per annum
• Monthly Payroll Disbursement Timeliness: 100% On-Time (By 25th of month)
• Statutory Compliance & HR Audit Rating: Grade A (Zero Non-Compliance)
• Disciplinary Case Resolution Window: < 14 Business Days
